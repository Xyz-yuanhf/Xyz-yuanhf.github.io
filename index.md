## About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Automation](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/index.htm), advised by Prof. [Shiji Song](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=rw6vWdcAAAAJ&hl=en). Before that, I received my B.E. degree in the [Department of Precision Instrument](https://www.dpi.tsinghua.edu.cn/) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/index.htm).

My research interests mainly lie at the interface of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Operational Research (OR). Specifically, my work is focused on exploiting [**Machine Learning**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning) (including Reinforcement Learning and Large Language Models) to solve [**Combinatorial Optimization**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_optimization) problems (such as Vehicle Routing Problem, Job-Shop Scheduling Problem, etc.) and accelerate algorithms (such as Branch & Bound, Column Generation, etc.) for solving large-scale [**Integer Programming**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer_programming) problems.


## Research Interests

- **Machine Learning:** reinforcement learning, graph neural network, large language model;
- **Combinatorial Optimization:** routing, scheduling, mixed integer linear programming;
- **Applications:** logistics and transportation, manufacturing and production, etc.
